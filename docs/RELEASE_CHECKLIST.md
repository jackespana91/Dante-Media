# Dante Media launch checklist — mandatory before publishing

- [ ] Confirm access to the actual dante-media.io hosting project and existing source/CMS.
- [ ] Capture current URLs, redirect patterns, analytics, imagery, film assets and existing metadata.
- [ ] Preserve approved site design and existing content that should survive the rebuild.
- [ ] Confirm all portfolio brand names, websites, operational statuses, trademark use, grouping and relationship language.
- [ ] Confirm whether advisory/consultancy is still offered and retain an appropriate landing page if so.
- [ ] Confirm Broken Homes legal form, country of registration and permissible foundation wording.
- [ ] Approve Broken Homes governance, funding/contribution policies, safeguarding and youth/mental-health boundaries.
- [ ] Document each joint venture's actual agreement or intention concerning Broken Homes contributions.
- [ ] Verify the group's official contact email, route into an authorised mailbox, and response ownership.
- [ ] Implement real legal notice, privacy/cookies disclosures, consent controls and required age/regulated gaming protections.
- [ ] Complete mobile, keyboard, screenreader, reduced motion, performance, broken-link and image-alt audits.
- [ ] Validate all canonicals, Open Graph, sitemap, structured data and redirects after integration.
- [ ] Review factual business claims, existing and planned ventures, and the absence of false impact statistics.
- [ ] Confirm cross-brand and partner links, consent, and brand rights.
- [ ] Test staging and rollback, get explicit publication approval, then publish and verify both www and apex hostnames.
