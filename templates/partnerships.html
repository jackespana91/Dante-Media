OUR EXPECTATIONS

Shared purpose.
Clear terms.

Business partnerships and joint ventures should have clearly documented commercial arrangements, intellectual property, governance and responsibilities.

We also want a suitable, agreed contribution to Broken Homes to become part of the relationship as the foundation becomes operational. Contributions can differ by partner—funding, skills, employment access, mentorship or other practical support.

Where children or vulnerable people could be involved, safeguarding and independent programme governance will always come before promotion, growth or commercial goals.
