OUR EXPECTATIONS

Shared purpose.
Clear terms.

The strongest partnerships give everyone a clear role, a shared direction and room to contribute what they do best. We shape each collaboration around the people, the product and the opportunity.

As Broken Homes develops, our ambition is for every group venture to contribute in a meaningful way. Funding, expertise, employment, mentoring and community connections can all help create opportunity. Each contribution will be shaped and agreed with the people involved.

For community partnerships, trusted local expertise and the wellbeing of the people involved will guide how we work together.
