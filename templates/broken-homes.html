THE REASON BEHIND THE WORK

To replace dead ends with people, possibilities and a genuine next step.

Some people grow up surrounded by instability, financial pressure, isolation, violence or simply a lack of opportunity. None of those circumstances should define the rest of a life.

Our ambition is to build a connected network of trusted local organisations, mentors, employers, educators, sports clubs and creative partners. By working with people who understand local needs, we want to help each person reach the right support and find a practical pathway forward.

Our long-term ambition is to help change thousands of lives through supportive relationships, stronger communities and access to opportunity.

THE FOCUS

Real help.
Real opportunities.

Our focus will be on practical routes forward, shaped with qualified local organisations and people who understand the communities they serve.

01 / GUIDANCE

Mentorship & belonging

Supportive relationships, positive role models and reliable routes to qualified help.

02 / PROGRESSION

Education & employment

Connections to training, apprenticeships, employment and development opportunities.

03 / PARTICIPATION

Sport, music & creativity

Places to belong, develop confidence and discover talents through community partners.

04 / SUPPORT

Local specialist networks

Collaboration with established organisations supporting families and young people affected by instability or disadvantage.

OUR GROUP-WIDE COMMITMENT
“Every business we build should help create opportunities beyond itself.”
THE BROKEN HOMES PRINCIPLE
EVERY BUSINESS · ITS OWN CONTRIBUTION

A purpose we share.
A future we build.

From group brands to collaborative joint ventures, our intention is to make social contribution a fundamental part of how new businesses are developed.

Some businesses may support the foundation financially. Others can offer access to clubs, careers, training, technical expertise, creators, venues, programmes or local networks.

Each business and venture will help shape its own contribution. As the foundation becomes operational, we intend to share how that support is used and the opportunities it creates.

Our principle: contribution should be practical, accountable and never dependent on promoting a commercial product.
