THE REASON BEHIND THE WORK

To replace dead ends with people, possibilities and a genuine next step.

Some people grow up surrounded by instability, financial pressure, isolation, violence or simply a lack of opportunity. None of those circumstances should define the rest of a life.

Our ambition is to build a connected network of trusted local organisations, mentors, employers, educators, sports clubs and creative partners. Rather than pretending one foundation can do everything, we want to help people reach the right support and open practical pathways forward.

We want to reach thousands of lives over time. That is an ambition for the future, not an outcome we claim to have achieved.

OUR GROUP-WIDE COMMITMENT
“Every business we build should help create opportunities beyond itself.”
THE BROKEN HOMES PRINCIPLE
EVERY BUSINESS · ITS OWN CONTRIBUTION

Not a side project.
A shared promise.

From group brands to collaborative joint ventures, our intention is to make social contribution a fundamental part of how new businesses are developed.

Some businesses may support the foundation financially. Others can offer access to clubs, careers, training, technical expertise, creators, venues, programmes or local networks.

The nature of those contributions will be agreed with each business or venture and documented appropriately. Once the foundation is operational, we intend to report real contributions and outcomes transparently.

Our principle: contribution should be practical, accountable and never dependent on promoting a commercial product.

Important: Broken Homes is not yet an active support service.

The foundation is being planned. This website does not provide live counselling, emergency assistance, or a referral service, and it should not be used to report an immediate safeguarding concern. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact their local emergency services or an established crisis service. Any future support involving children or vulnerable people will require appropriate safeguarding, privacy arrangements and qualified delivery partners.

Commercial independence: Access to future support must never require gambling activity, marketing consent or participation in promotional content. Information about beneficiaries must be kept separate from commercial and gaming systems. Gaming brands must not market to children or use foundation beneficiaries in acquisition activity.
