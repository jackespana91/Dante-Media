THE DANTE MEDIA MODEL

Built to create.
Made to matter.

We create, collaborate and help promising ideas grow, with the freedom to develop a distinct identity and purpose.

HOW WE THINK

Independent identities.
Shared experience.
One common commitment.

Dante Media brings together founders, creatives, developers, operators and commercial talent across different industries. Some initiatives are built internally; others are collaborations and joint ventures.

We want every venture to be recognised for what makes it distinctive. The group's role is to connect the right people, experience and commercial opportunities while giving each brand room to grow.

Broken Homes is the commitment we plan to place alongside that work, so that success can help create opportunity for people who need it.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Four ideas.
One direction.

01 / BUILD

Start with substance

Build useful products, distinctive experiences and credible ventures around the people they serve.

02 / COLLABORATE

Bring people together

Make space for different teams, creative styles, local expertise and commercial partnerships.

03 / STAND APART

Respect every identity

Protect the individual identities, intellectual property and governance of businesses and partner studios.

04 / GIVE BACK

Make contribution real

Develop documented ways for each business to support Broken Homes as the foundation takes shape.

FROM IDEA TO INITIATIVE

A clear path.
Room to evolve.

Every venture follows its own route. These principles help us keep ambition grounded in what a team can build, prove and sustain.

01 / DISCOVER

Start with a real need

Understand the audience, the opportunity and the people closest to the problem before deciding what to build.

02 / SHAPE

Give the idea its own identity

Define the product, team, brand and responsibilities so each initiative has a clear reason to exist.

03 / DEVELOP

Build with the right partners

Bring together the creative, technical and commercial skills needed to move the work forward responsibly.

04 / CONTRIBUTE

Connect success to purpose

Plan a meaningful contribution to Broken Homes as the foundation and its programmes take shape.
