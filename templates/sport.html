FEATURED VENTURE · IN DEVELOPMENT

Futpal.
Everyone has a place.

Futpal aims to connect players, parents, coaches, clubs, leagues, associations, referees and grassroots football communities on one platform.

Alongside organisation and discovery tools, the broader vision is to make sport more accessible and help people find a community, opportunities to develop and routes to appropriate support.

As Futpal develops, there is a natural opportunity for carefully governed community partnerships that align with Broken Homes.
